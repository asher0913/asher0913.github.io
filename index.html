DualPath-CEM

Nov 2025 – Jun 2026

Final-year thesis · privacy-preserving split inference · DualPathCEM · SlotCEM

  • Designed a dual-path split-inference architecture that transmits a Gaussian-protected 16×16×16 SlotCEM spatial tensor and a separately protected 256-D MobileNetV3 semantic token, then combines server-side predictions through calibrated logit fusion.
  • Developed a class-conditional SlotCEM regularizer that projects 4,096-D smashed features into a 64-D slot space using 8 slots, 3 refinement iterations, and a 64-entry per-class memory bank across 526 identities.
  • Reached 81.96% mean top-1 on a 44,139-image FaceScrub split across five adaptation seeds, 1.63 points above the published Noise_ARL+CEM reference, while increasing decoder and GAN reconstruction MSE by 75.8% and 74.9%.

Manuscript in preparation: Y. Zhang and J. Ren, “DDP-CEM: Dual-Path Privacy Protection for Collaborative Face Inference,” Pattern Recognition, 2026.
